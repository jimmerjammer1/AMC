Questions

Before you reach out.

Is $500 really the whole design cost?

Yes. $500 covers a custom-designed site of up to five pages, built for your business and ready to launch. If you need something larger — a store, a members' area — we'll tell you the extra cost up front before any work starts.

What does the $100/month SEO plan actually include?

Ongoing on-page optimization, technical health checks, local search setup, and a monthly report showing what changed and why. It's the slow, steady work that gets you found — not a one-time trick.

Do I need to sign a contract?

No. The $500 design fee is one-time. The $100/month SEO plan is month-to-month — cancel anytime, no penalty.

How long does the site take to build?

Most sites launch within about a week of our discovery call, depending on how quickly we get your content and feedback.

Do you handle my domain and hosting?

We'll guide you through picking a domain and hosting if you don't already have one, and handle the technical setup so your site goes live without any guesswork on your end.
